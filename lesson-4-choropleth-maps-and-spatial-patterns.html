Key word: choropleth map

Definition

A map that shades each area (here, each country) by its value, using a key of groups.

How to read it

Darker shading = a higher group. Lighter or patterned = a lower group. Patterns as well as colours help when it is printed in black and white.

What it shows well

Spatial variation: differences between countries and regions, and where the high and low groups are clustered.

What it does NOT show

Differences inside a country. Each country gets one colour for its national average.

Step 1: Read the map first

Source: map drawn for this lesson from UNDP, Human Development Report 2025 (data for 2023). Country outlines: Natural Earth (public domain).

Read it like a geographer

  1. Title: what, where, when?
  2. Key: how many groups? Which colour is highest? Which is lowest?
  3. No data: which countries have no value?
  4. Labels: which countries are named?

Model answer: General, Examples, Exceptions

Describe the spatial pattern of life expectancy shown on the map. Refer to at least two regions of the world. (3 marks)

Life expectancy varies greatly around the world: it is highest in wealthy regions and lowest in sub-Saharan Africa. Most of Western Europe, as well as Japan, Australia, New Zealand and Canada, is in the highest group (80 years and over), whereas many countries in western and central Africa, such as Nigeria, are in the lowest group (under 65 years). However, there are exceptions: the United States is in a lower group (75–79.9 years) than Canada, and Russia (65–74.9 years) is lower than the rest of Europe.

(b) Sample 3-mark answer

Income per person is highest in North America, Western Europe, Japan and South Korea, Australia and New Zealand, and lowest in sub-Saharan Africa. Most of these wealthy regions, and some Middle East countries such as Saudi Arabia, are in the highest group (US$40,000 and over), whereas most countries in sub-Saharan Africa, such as Niger, are in the lowest group (under US$5,000). However, Guyana is an exception: it is in the highest group, while most of South America is in the two middle groups.
